Under **System → Users** you manage the people who log in to your site's panel.

<Aside type="note">
This guide has no screenshot yet: the one we have shows the team's real emails. One with demo data will be added.
</Aside>

## Roles

- **Administrators** manage the whole site.
- **Editors** manage content, but **not** settings, sales, customers or users.

The screen also shows a **Professional** role, meant for whoever attends appointments: it can be **linked to a professional** in the schedule.

## What you will see

Each user appears with their name, their email and the date they have been on the site since. On the right:

- A selector to change their **role**.
- A button with a **key** icon to manage their access.
- The **trash can** to remove them.

To add someone, click **New user**.

<Aside type="tip">
Give each person the minimum role they need. Someone who only writes blog articles, for example, can be an editor.
</Aside>
