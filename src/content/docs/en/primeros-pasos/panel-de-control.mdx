The **Dashboard** (*Panel de control*) is the first thing you see when you log in. It sums up how your site is doing and takes you to every section.

<Aside type="note">
This guide has no screenshot yet: the one we have shows real figures from a business. One with demo data will be added.
</Aside>

## The menu on the left

Everything is grouped into four blocks. Click a block to expand it:

| Block | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **Content** | [Pages](/en/contenido/paginas/), Products, Testimonials, [Menu](/en/contenido/menu/), [Blog](/en/contenido/blog/) and [Media](/en/contenido/media/). |
| **Sales and customers** | [Orders](/en/ventas/pedidos/), [Purchases and sales](/en/ventas/compras-y-ventas/), [Customers](/en/ventas/clientes/), [Forms](/en/ventas/formularios/) and Support tickets. |
| **System** | [Chatbot](/en/sistema/chatbot/), [Calendar](/en/sistema/calendario/), [Email](/en/sistema/correo/), [Campaigns](/en/sistema/campanas/), [Users](/en/sistema/usuarios/), [Plan](/en/primeros-pasos/planes-y-limites/), API connection and [Settings](/en/sistema/configuracion/). |
| **Platform** | Tools specific to the platform. This guide does not cover them yet. |

If you manage several sites, the **site selector** is at the top of the menu: it shows the name and domain of the active site and lets you switch between them.

At the top right you have **View site**, which opens your public site, and the **notifications** bell.

## Visitor behavior

The first card shows what your visitors did over the **last 30 days**. Measurement is **anonymous and cookie-free**:

- Product views, adds to cart, checkouts started and orders created.
- Clicks on social networks and clicks on blog advertising (by type, and the articles with the most clicks).
- Times the chatbot was opened.
- Where product views come from and roughly how many sessions there were.

## Visits, revenue and orders

Four cards compare the **last 14 days** with the 14 before: **Visits**, **Revenue**, **Orders** and **New messages**. A green percentage means it went up, red means it went down.

Below there are counters for what you have loaded (products, testimonials, modules…) and a chart of visits per day.

## Plan limits

To the right of the chart, progress bars show how much of your plan you are using: emails sent this month, Media storage, forms, blog articles, and so on. When a bar fills up, it is time to review your [plan](/en/primeros-pasos/planes-y-limites/).

## Latest activity

At the bottom, the latest **sales (income)** and **stock purchases (expenses)**, with date, description, status (**Completed** or **Pending**) and amount. **See all** opens [Purchases and sales](/en/ventas/compras-y-ventas/).
