**Sales and customers → Customers** is a small CRM: each customer's contact details, their order, form and ticket history, and your internal notes.

<Aside type="note">
This guide has no screenshots yet: the ones we have show real customer data. They will be added with demo data.
</Aside>

## The list

- The **Active** and **Deleted** tabs show how many customers are in each group.
- The search filters by **name, email or phone**.
- Each customer shows how many **orders**, **forms** and **tickets** they have. Click their row to open their record.

## Add a customer

<Steps>

1. Click **Add customer**.
2. Fill in **Email**, **Full name**, **Phone** and **Address**.
3. Click **Create customer**.

</Steps>

They are added to the site's CRM and respect your [plan](/en/primeros-pasos/planes-y-limites/)'s customer limit.

## The customer record

When you open a customer you will see:

- **Customer details:** name, email, phone, avatar URL and address, plus the date they became a customer. Change what you need and click **Save**.
- **Internal notes:** write a note and click **Add note**. **Only the administrator team sees them**; the customer never has access.

## Remove a customer

The **Remove customer** button blocks their access to the portal and takes them off your active list, but **keeps their account and their whole history** (orders, forms, tickets and notes). You can restore them whenever you like from the **Deleted** tab, with their history just as it was.

## Customer sign-up

Your customers can create their own account on your site: see [Customer portal](/en/ventas/portal-del-cliente/). If you prefer that only you create accounts, turn off sign-up in [Settings → Customers](/en/sistema/configuracion/).
