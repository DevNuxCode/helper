Under **Sales and customers → Orders** you see all the orders generated from your store's cart.

## What each order shows

Each order is a card with:

- The customer's name and the **total**.
- The date, the number of products and the **tracking code** (`PED-…`).
- The detail of **products**, **shipping** and the customer's data (phone and email).
- The **document** (receipt or invoice) and the **delivery** (pickup or home delivery, with its address and notes).

## Change the status

On the right of each order there is a status selector (for example, **Completed**). When you change it, the customer can see the new status on their order tracking page and in their account.

You can also **delete** the order with the red button.

## Courier tracking

When you ship, the **Courier tracking** block lets you send the link to the customer:

1. In **Tracking URL (without the number)** paste the courier's address, without the tracking number at the end.
2. In **Tracking no.** type the number the courier gave you.
3. Click **Save tracking**.
4. Click **Send tracking email** to let the customer know.

The two parts are joined to form the link the customer gets in the email and sees on their order page.

<Aside type="tip">
You can customize the text of the tracking email under **Email → Settings**.
</Aside>
