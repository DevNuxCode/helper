Every form you put on your pages (bookings, demo request, contact) leaves its responses under **Sales and customers → Forms**.

<Aside type="note">
This guide has no screenshot yet: the one we have shows real contact data. One with demo data will be added.
</Aside>

## What you will see

Each submission is a card with:

- The **form name** (for example, "Booking form" or "Demo request") and the date and time it arrived.
- The **fields** the person filled in: time, date, name, service, phone, etc.
- A **WhatsApp** label when the submission came through that channel.
- A status selector and the **Delete** button.

## Statuses

Change the status with each submission's selector:

| Status | What for |
|---|---|
| **New** | You have not reviewed it yet. |
| **Read** | You have seen it. |

## Filter

At the top right, the **All forms** selector lets you see only the submissions of one particular form.

## How to create a form

Forms are added as **modules** on a page with the [Visual editor](/en/contenido/editor-visual/): choose **Form** or **Form with CPA** in **Add module**. Booking-type forms also appear in the [Calendar](/en/sistema/calendario/).
